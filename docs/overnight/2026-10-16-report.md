## Shipped (merged to main, CI green on all three jobs, separate adversarial review)

- **Paste precision (B1, #49).** The target (app, window, focused field, browser page) is captured
  at the press and the text goes back there, or it's copied with a notice. Text with line breaks is
  never typed into a type-out app (it would run a terminal command); it's copied.
- **App-aware formatting (B2/B3, #51 core, #53 wiring, #57 docs).** Email, Work chat, Personal chat,
  Coding, Docs and notes, Other, decided at the press from the app or the browser page, with
  per-category styles, your name for sign-offs, per-app overrides and an opt-in send key. Up to 200
  characters before the cursor are read to continue the sentence, kept in memory for that dictation
  only.

  `evals/results/2026-10-16-cleanup.md`. Rules lost 0 spoken words in-sample and held-out; S1-mini

## Calls I made that you may want to change

- The hotkey with Useful Voice in front pastes only into a focused editable field of ours (a note,
  the first-run Try it pad); otherwise it's copied. Window and own-field dictations get no app
  formatting and never a send key.
- Auto-send is off everywhere. When on, it also fires after a paste that couldn't be proven (most
  chat apps can't be read), always after a fresh check that the same field still has focus.
- `com.openai.codex` formats as Work chat, because the ChatGPT Mac app ships under that id
  (chatgpt.com/codex in a browser stays Coding).
- A filler's comma is dropped mid-clause and kept after greetings, answer words and intro phrases.
- The S1-mini RAM rule is "at least 7 GiB reported" on both platforms: Windows reports 8 GB machines
  as 7.x GiB.
- A cold model never holds a dictation: the first dictation after launch or idle gets the rules text
  while the model loads.
- History records the app and category of new dictations (additive, nothing rewritten); I judged that
  not risky and merged it with #53.
