**DISMISSED**

- **Command injection:** transcript and clipboard contents aren't interpolated into PowerShell commands. Cue paths escape single quotes. Interpolated window handles require positive safe integers.
- **Sentinel collisions:** completion requires the current random UUID. Arbitrary window-title output cannot predict it. A title containing a newline followed by `UVERR:` can fail the foreground query, but that yields `null` and preserves the clipboard fallback.
- **Late output after request timeout:** teardown increments the generation before replacement. Events from the retired child are ignored.
- **Automatic duplicate paste on restart:** `runHosted` retries only requests marked unsent. Requests that may have executed aren't replayed. Aborted queued requests are skipped before writing.
- **Concurrent dictations:** the service’s busy/session guards prevent a second recording from starting during delivery.
- **Reverted-code leftovers:** no remaining active references to the removed app-awareness or cleanup runtimes were found. `ownProcessName` is an unused export without a runtime effect. No new API-key exposure was found.
