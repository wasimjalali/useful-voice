Fixes:
- macOS 40a7273: Now Playing is read through Apple's /usr/bin/perl loading our own helper dylib (bundle/mediaremote/uv_nowplaying.c + uv-nowplaying.pl, built into Contents/Resources/MediaRemote, signed by make bundle / bundle-release), via one long-lived perl process (NowPlayingReader, 500 ms read timeout, gives up after 3 failures). Pause only when Now Playing is playing; remember its pid+bundle; resume only if Now Playing is still that app, paused and untouched (500 ms watch); never Play with no Now Playing app (it would launch Music); Play landing on another app is paused again. Core Audio stays as fallback with per-kind stop windows (Chromium/Electron helpers up to 10.5 s, held pending; their silence tail never counts as touched; Music/Spotify/WebKit.GPU 300 ms; others 100 ms).
- Windows 204081b: quitCoordinator.ts: session-end starts the resume then app.quit() so the app is quitting; before-quit lets Electron's quit through; will-quit is held with preventDefault while the orderly quit resumes media and ends in app.exit; a second app.quit() is held; per-session UVSKIP keeps slow pauses held as unconfirmed; resume requires the UVRESUMED sentinel; behavioural quit tests.
