What you get.

  • 01

    It lands where you started.

    Useful Voice remembers the app and the field you were in when you pressed the key. Switch windows while it transcribes and the text still goes back there. If that field is gone, the text waits on your clipboard instead of landing somewhere else.

  • 02

    Written for the app you're in.

    Email gets a greeting, paragraphs and a sign-off. Chat stays casual. Docs stay formal. It knows Gmail, Slack, Notion and more, in the browser too, and you can set any app's style in Settings.

  • 03

    Speaks code.

    In Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and terminals, “index dot ts” becomes index.ts, “tag index dot ts” becomes @index.ts and “dash dash verbose” becomes --verbose. Identifiers stay exactly as you said them, and terminals that block paste get the text typed out.

  • 04

    The ums come out.

    Fillers and stutters are removed before the text lands, on your device. Cleanup only deletes: it never adds words or rewrites what you meant. Carry on mid-sentence and the first word stays lowercase.

  • 05

    Teach it your words.

    Add names and specialist spellings to your dictionary, with a “sounds like” form for words it mishears. Fix a recurring mistake once and it stays fixed.

  • 06

    Nothing you say gets lost.

    Every dictation is copied to your clipboard and saved in Dictations, so a missed insert is one paste away. Keep searchable notes, and see your words and time saved in Insights.

Before you install.

What leaves your computer, in full: the privacy page.

  • Works onmacOS 14 or later on Apple silicon. Windows 10 or later, 64-bit
  • EnginesDeepgram Nova-3 with your own key: new Deepgram accounts get $200 in free credit, no card needed, which is 500+ hours of dictation. On a Mac, also Whisper large-v3-turbo or large-v3, running on your Mac
  • LanguagesBuilt and tested for English first. Both engines also detect and transcribe many other languages
  • HotkeyRight Command on a Mac, Ctrl+Alt+Space on Windows. Change it in Settings
  • Stays on your deviceYour dictionary, notes, dictation history and settings. Cleanup and formatting run locally. Your Deepgram key sits in the macOS Keychain (Windows: encrypted with DPAPI)
  • Leaves your deviceWith Deepgram, each recording's audio and up to 400 tokens of your dictionary words. With a local Whisper model, nothing: the model downloads once from Hugging Face
  • TrackingNone. No analytics, no crash reports, no ads
  • UpdatesIn the app, from the public releases on GitHub
  • Price and sourceFree and open source under the MIT license. Read the source
