Privacy

Useful Voice is a dictation app for Mac and Windows. Your dictations, notes and dictionary live on your computer, and we never see them. This page lists everything the app sends, where it goes and why.

  • We receiveNothing. The app has no account and sends us no data

What stays on your computer

Your dictation history, notes, dictionary, snippets and settings are stored on your computer, in your user folders. On a Mac, the app also keeps your last 10 recordings by default, so you can retry one. You can change that in Settings. We have no copy and no account system.

Cleaning up fillers and formatting the text for the app you're in happen on your computer. To do that, and to put the text back in the right place, the app notes which app and window you were in when you pressed the key and, in a browser, the page address. That stays on your computer: the app's name and its category are saved with each dictation in your history.

The app also keeps a short, capped log of errors and events on your computer (Settings › Diagnostics). It holds brief error descriptions, never your dictations or your Deepgram key. Nothing sends it anywhere unless you copy it yourself.

Your Deepgram key is stored in the macOS Keychain on a Mac, and encrypted with Windows DPAPI on Windows.

On a Mac, the app asks for two permissions. Microphone records only while you dictate. Accessibility lets it paste into the field you were in and read the text just before your cursor, so it knows whether you're mid-sentence.

What leaves your computer, and why

Deepgram, if you use it

With Deepgram Nova-3 as your engine, each recording's audio goes to Deepgram to be transcribed, with up to 400 tokens of your dictionary words, fixes and snippet triggers as hints. “Sounds like” forms stay on your computer. The request uses your own Deepgram key, so Deepgram handles it under your account and its own terms and privacy policy. Checking a new key also makes one request to Deepgram.

Local models

On a Mac you can transcribe with a Whisper model that runs on your Mac instead. Then your audio never leaves it. The model downloads once from Hugging Face, which sees your IP address and the file you fetch, as with any download.

Updates

The app checks the public releases on GitHub for new versions. GitHub sees your IP address and the app version, as with any download. On a Mac, every update is checked against the app's signing key before it installs.

Links you open

Links in the app, such as Deepgram's signup page or Report a bug on GitHub, open in your browser. What you do there is between you and that site.

What we don't do

No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting in the app. No ads or cross-site tracking. We don't sell data and we don't train AI models on anything you say.

This website

voice.usefulbuild.com is served by Cloudflare, which processes technical request data such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. This site runs no analytics and sets no cookies. Downloads come from GitHub.

Your rights

We hold no data about you from the app. If you email us, we keep the conversation until it's no longer needed. If you're in the EEA or the UK, you can ask to see or delete it, or object to how we use it, at hello@usefulbuild.com, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

If what the app sends changes, we update this page and its date first.
